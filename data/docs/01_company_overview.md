# NovaCart - Company Overview

## Mission
NovaCart delivers a seamless online shopping experience with reliable fulfillment and exceptional customer satisfaction.

## Company Profile
- Founded: 2019
- Headquarters: Austin, TX
- Employees: ~520
- Fulfillment Centers: 3 (Austin-TX, Newark-NJ, Reno-NV)
- Monthly Order Volume: 50,000-200,000
- Active SKUs: ~25,000
- Operating Regions: USA and Canada

## Org Structure
Operations falls under the COO and includes: Fulfillment, Inventory Management, Quality Assurance, Supply Chain, and Business Operations Analysis.
