# SOP-004: Monthly Ops Reporting
Version: 1.2 | Last Updated: 2025-11-01

## Purpose
Produce consistent monthly operations reports for leadership review.

## Schedule
- Data compilation: 1st-3rd business day of the month
- Draft review: 4th business day
- Final submission to VP Operations: 5th business day

## Report Contents
1. **Executive Summary** (1 page): Key takeaways, top issues, recommendations
2. **KPI Dashboard**: Monthly actuals vs targets vs prior month
3. **Variance Analysis**: >10% variance requires explanation
4. **Incident Summary**: All incidents with severity Medium+
5. **Top 3 Issues**: Current month's critical issues with action plan
6. **Next Month Forecast**: Expected volume, known risks

## Report Template
Standard template is maintained in the Ops SharePoint. Each section has defined data sources and responsible owner.
