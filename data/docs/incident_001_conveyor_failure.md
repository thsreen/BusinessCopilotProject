# Incident Report: Warehouse B Conveyor Failure

## Incident ID: INC-2026-001
- Date: June 12, 2026
- Duration: 6 hours (09:00-15:00 CT)
- Severity: High
- Reported By: David Kim (Director of Fulfillment)

## Timeline
- 08:45 -- Conveyor belt motor reported running hot
- 09:00 -- Motor seized, belt stopped. Full stoppage of packing line 2
- 09:15 -- Maintenance team dispatched
- 10:30 -- Diagnosis: motor burnout (bearing failure), no spare available
- 11:00 -- Emergency order placed with supplier (2hr delivery)
- 13:00 -- Replacement motor arrived
- 14:00 -- Installation complete
- 15:00 -- Line back at full capacity

## Impact
- 2,500 orders delayed (avg +6 hours)
- Fulfillment time for Warehouse B: 45 hrs avg on June 12
- No customer-facing impact (all orders still delivered within committed window)

## Root Cause
Motor bearing reached end of life. No preventative replacement schedule. No spare motor kept onsite.

## Action Items
1. [Done] Order 2 spare motors for each warehouse
2. [Done] Implement quarterly motor bearing inspection
3. [In Progress] Create preventative maintenance schedule for all conveyor systems
4. [Done] Add motor temperature monitoring sensors
