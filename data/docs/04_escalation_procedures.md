# Escalation Procedures

## Tier Definitions
- **Tier 1 (Team Lead):** KPI breach < 2 days, single incident
- **Tier 2 (Operations Manager):** KPI breach 2+ consecutive days, or incident with moderate impact
- **Tier 3 (COO / VP Ops):** KPI breach 3+ days, critical impact, revenue impact > $50K

## When to Escalate
1. Any critical threshold breach lasting 2+ days
2. Any safety or compliance incident
3. Customer-facing outage > 30 minutes
4. Inventory variance > 5% in any category
5. Supplier quality failure affecting > 1,000 units

## Escalation Process
1. Incident reporter fills the escalation template
2. Tier-1 lead triages within 30 minutes
3. If unresolved in 2 hours -> Tier-2
4. If unresolved in 6 hours -> Tier-3 (with COO notification)
