# Reporting Calendar

| Frequency | Meeting | Attendees | Data Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily | Morning Ops Standup (9:00 AM CT) | Ops team | Previous day KPIs, incidents, anomalies |
| Weekly | Weekly Ops Review (Monday 10:00 AM) | Ops + Product | Weekly trends, top issues, action items |
| Monthly | Monthly Business Review (1st Wed) | Leadership | Full KPI deck, financials, strategic |
| Quarterly | Quarterly Ops Planning | All departments | Deep dives, initiatives, resource planning |

## Daily Standup Format
1. KPI snapshot (5 KPIs: volume, fulfillment, returns, wait time, CSAT)
2. Anomalies from last 24 hours
3. Active escalations
4. Priorities for today
